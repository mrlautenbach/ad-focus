Features

One place for the whole athletics program, on Mac with a companion iPhone app.

AD Focus is organised into modules that share the same events, people and venues. A change in one place carries through to the others, so a rescheduled game updates the officials, transport, facilities and budget that depend on it.

Start each day knowing what needs you

  • Morning briefOne line for each module that needs action: weather, officials, transport, clearances, certifications, facilities, budget, urgent mail and follow-ups. Each line opens where it can be fixed.
  • Today and week planningGoals and seasons turn into weekly focus blocks, with a game-day timeline and an end-of-day wrap-up.
  • Command palettePress ⌘K to jump to any event, person or module and run actions on the spot.
  • iPhone TodayThe same brief on your phone, with a widget, a morning notification and quick incident logging.

Plan the season

  • Athletics calendarGames, practices and venue bookings in one view. Drag to reschedule, type an event in plain words, and get warned when two entries look like the same event.
  • Conflict detectionClashes between venues, teams and staff are flagged early.
  • Year planner and season runwaySee the whole year, and work back from key dates to close-out.
  • Teams, people and venuesA page for each team, person and venue, gathering everything connected to them.
  • Schedule pagesPublish schedules as web pages for families and coaches.
  • Google CalendarOptionally show busy times and publish approved events, only after you confirm each change.

Run game day

  • Event hubOne sheet per event with follow-ups, weather, officials, travel, venue, roles, to-dos, checklist, budget and incidents.
  • Game-day checklists and scriptsStaffing, set-up and announcer scripts for each event.
  • Weather policySet the thresholds your school uses and get alerts and suggested actions when a game is affected.
  • Change cascadeWhen a game moves or is cancelled, the people and bookings it touches are listed for you to review.
  • OfficialsAssign officials, send requests, and track accepted and declined responses from a simple web link.
  • Scoring and resultsRecord results and keep season records.

Travel, facilities and equipment

  • Trip plannerPlan travel, travellers and eligibility, with flight-status checks and a trip risk review.
  • Facilities schedulingBook fields and courts, resolve overlaps, and track inspections.
  • EquipmentTeam boards with check-out and check-in, saved sizes, group checkout, and season equipment.
  • Library loansLend books and resources to coaches by tag or ISBN scan, with overdue lists and email drafts.
  • CateringA matrix of needs, sessions, caterers and orders, with a serve desk for the day.

People, safety and communication

  • Coaches and staffCertifications, clearances, onboarding, evaluations and staff notes. Evaluations are visible to the Athletic Director only.
  • Athletes and rostersImport rosters, track participation, and archive at season end.
  • Incident logRecord incidents, follow up, and manage return-to-play. Schools can switch the module off.
  • Emergency action plansVenue plans kept ready where staff can find them.
  • MailA Gmail inbox inside the app with a split view, live search, reply-later and set-aside piles, and sender context. Mail from a sender can become an event or an incident.
  • Surveys and noticesDrafts for families and staff. Nothing is sent without your approval, except surveys you choose to auto-send.

Budget and goals

  • BudgetLines, buckets, routing for approval and needs review, with an over-budget filter and a source breakdown.
  • Income and boostersTrack fundraising and booster contributions alongside spending.
  • GoalsLink goals to the work that moves them.
  • AD BrainA workspace for notes and plans that connect back to your events and tasks.
  • TasksOptionally sync with Google Tasks or Things.

For your whole department

  • Team editionStaff sign in with their school Google account. Roles are Athletic Director, Assistant AD, Coach, Staff and Read-only, and the Athletic Director sets them.
  • Live syncChanges appear for the rest of the team as they happen.
  • Coach viewCoaches land on their own teams and their own Today page.
  • Guided setupA start-up checklist, a tutorial for staff joining a school, and clear empty states.
  • Custom sidebarA recommended layout grouped by Planning, Athletics, Operations, People, Communication and Admin, which you can rearrange.

Assistance that stays in your control

AI features run only when you click them. They can use Apple's on-device model or a cloud provider you choose. Student names are redacted before anything leaves the device. Notices are drafts for you to review and are never sent automatically. See the Privacy Policy for details.
