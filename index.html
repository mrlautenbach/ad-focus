AD Focus

The planning app for school Athletic Directors, made for macOS.

AD Focus helps Athletic Directors and their staff run an athletics program without losing sight of the work that matters. It brings seasons, game and practice schedules, venues, staffing and day-to-day tasks together in one place. It flags conflicts early and helps you plan each week around your real priorities.

What it does

  • Athletics calendarGames, practices and venue bookings in one view, with automatic conflict detection.
  • Weekly planningGoals and seasons become weekly focus blocks, and the inbox is triaged by priority.
  • Game-day operationsChecklists, staffing, and disruption handling for weather and cancellations.
  • Calendar linkingOptionally connect Google Calendar to see busy times and publish approved events.
  • Task syncOptionally sync tasks with Google Tasks or Things.
  • Team editionStaff sign in with Google. Roles and permissions are set by the Athletic Director.

How AD Focus uses Google data

AD Focus uses Google Sign-In so staff can log in with their school account. If you choose to connect Google services, AD Focus uses:

  • Google Calendar to show busy times and venue bookings from the calendars you link. It only writes events to calendars where you have turned publishing on, and only after you review and confirm each change.
  • Google Tasks to keep your AD Focus tasks and your Google Tasks lists in sync.

Google data is used only to provide these features to you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
