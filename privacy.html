Privacy Policy

AD Focus · Last updated October 11, 2026

Summary

  • AD Focus is a planning app for school athletics staff. We collect only what the app needs to work.
  • We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Google data you connect is used only to provide AD Focus features to you. It is never used to train AI models.
  • AI features are optional. They run only when you ask, and student names are removed by default. On supported Macs, everyday AI tasks run on your Mac and nothing is sent anywhere.

Who we are

AD Focus is published by My World Discovered ("we", "us"). You can reach us at myworlddiscovered@gmail.com.

When a school or organisation uses AD Focus, that organisation decides what information goes into the app and who can see it. For that information, the organisation is the data controller and we act as its data processor.

Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We use these to identify you, link you to your organisation, and show who did what in the app.

Information you enter

This includes teams, seasons, schedules, venues, tasks, goals, notes, staff assignments, and team rosters. Rosters are limited to student name and grade. AD Focus has no fields for medical, health, or government ID information, and you should not enter that kind of information.

Activity history

AD Focus keeps an audit log of changes, recording who changed what and when, so mistakes can be undone. Audit entries are deleted automatically after 30 days.

What we do not collect

We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking technology. We also do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing activity.

Google user data

AD Focus asks for access to Google services only when you choose to connect them:

AccessWhat AD Focus does with it
Google Sign-In (name, email, profile picture)Signs you in and links you to your organisation.
Google CalendarReads events from the calendars you link so it can show busy times, games, practices and venue bookings, and check for conflicts. It creates, updates or removes events only on calendars where you have turned publishing on, and only after you preview and confirm the change.
Google TasksReads and updates your task lists to keep them in sync with AD Focus tasks.

AD Focus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We use Google data only to provide and improve the AD Focus features you can see in the app.
  • We do not sell Google data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not use Google data to develop, improve, or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your Google data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security or legal reasons, or the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.
  • Google data is shared with a cloud AI provider only when you start an AI request that uses one, and only the information shown in that request (see below). Requests handled by the on-device model never leave your Mac.

You can disconnect Google access at any time in AD Focus Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Apple Calendar, Reminders and Things

If you allow it, AD Focus reads and writes Apple Calendar and Reminders data, and syncs with the Things app. All of this happens on your Mac and is not sent to us.

AI features (optional)

AD Focus can draft weekly plans, briefs and messages with AI. It can use two kinds of AI:

  • On your Mac: on Macs that support Apple Intelligence, everyday tasks such as briefs, message drafts, chat and tidying pasted notes run on Apple's on-device model. These requests are processed entirely on your Mac and are not sent to us, to Apple, or to anyone else. This is on by default where available, and you can turn it off in Settings › AI.
  • A cloud provider you choose: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google (Gemini), or another compatible service. Weekly planning and reviews always use this, and so do other tasks if you turn the on-device option off. You supply your own API key, which is kept in your Mac's Keychain.

For both kinds:

  • AI requests run only when you start them. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • You can see exactly what will be sent before you send it.
  • Student names are removed by default. Sensitive staff notes are included only if you choose to include them for that request.
  • Cloud requests go directly from your Mac to the provider you picked, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store them.
  • Drafts are never sent to anyone without your action.

Where data is stored

  • On your Mac: app data and settings. Sign-in tokens and API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • Team edition: shared organisation data is stored with Supabase in the European Union. Each organisation's data is kept separate, and access is controlled by roles set by your Athletic Director.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run AD Focus:

  • Supabase for database hosting and sign-in (EU region).
  • Google for sign-in, plus Calendar and Tasks if you connect them.
  • Your chosen cloud AI provider, only for AI requests you start that use it. On-device AI requests do not involve any provider.

We do not share personal data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep data

  • Organisation data is kept while the organisation uses AD Focus.
  • Audit log entries are deleted after 30 days.
  • When an organisation ends its use of AD Focus, it can export its data. We then delete the data from our systems within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or to object to or limit how it is used. Because your organisation controls most of the data in AD Focus, please contact your Athletic Director or school first. You can also email us, and we will help or pass your request to your organisation. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Students and children

AD Focus is meant for adult school staff. Students do not use it or sign in to it. Staff may enter limited student information (name and grade) for team rosters. The school is responsible for having the right to do so under its own policies and applicable laws, such as FERPA in the United States.

Security

We protect data with encryption, separate data for each organisation, role-based access, and secure storage of secrets in the Keychain. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify affected organisations without undue delay.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests: myworlddiscovered@gmail.com.
