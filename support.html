About AD Focus

What AD Focus is, who uses it, and what you need to run it.

Who uses it

AD Focus is a planner for school Athletic Directors and their staff: one Mac app, with an iPhone companion, for the calendar, teams, game days, trips, budget, equipment, people and athletics mail.

Each school has one Athletic Director (AD) who creates it and invites everyone else. Every person has one role:

RoleTypical personWhat they can do
ADAthletic DirectorEverything, including invites, roles and deleting the school
Assistant ADDeputy, coordinatorAlmost everything; Coach Onboarding on by default
CoachHead or assistant coachTheir own teams; many screens read-only
StaffOffice or facilities staffThe modules the AD switches on for them
Read-onlyObservers, administratorsView and export only

The AD can change what any role, or any single person, can use in Settings › Team › Feature Access….

Requirements

  • A Mac running macOS 15 or later.
  • A Google account (usually your school Google Workspace account) to sign in to a school. AD Focus has no separate passwords.
  • An internet connection for sharing with your team. The app works offline and catches up when the connection returns.
  • Optional: an iPhone for the companion app and widgets.

Where your data lives, and licences

Each Mac keeps its own copy. A school's shared data is stored in a secure database in the EU, encrypted in transit and at rest. Each person sees only what their role allows, and every change is logged.

Mail is never shared: your Gmail stays on your own Mac. See the Privacy Policy.

Each school holds an annual licence sized by seats; a seat is one invited or active person. A new school starts with a 30-day free trial, one trial per Google account.

Installing and first launch

Most people are running within ten minutes: install, open, choose how to start, sign in with Google.

Install and open AD Focus

  1. Open the AD Focus download and drag AD Focus into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. Only one copy runs at a time; opening it again brings the open copy to the front.

Choose how to start

The Welcome window offers three choices.

  • Set up a new school — for the AD. It walks through School (name, time zone, week start, levels) → Your department (which modules the school uses) → Season names → First year (dates, seasons, blackouts) → Teams & venues → Import data → Review. Each step saves as you continue, so stopping part-way keeps what's done.
  • Join my school — for everyone invited by an AD. It opens Settings › Team. Click Sign In to Team, sign in with the Google account the invitation was sent to, then click Join. Only join schools you know.
  • Just look around — loads a sample school (6 sports, 12 teams, 3 venues and a season of games). Only offered on a Mac that isn't connected to a school yet.

Closed the Welcome window by mistake? Open File › School Setup… or Help › School Setup….

Get started checklist and importing data

Settings › Get Started lists every setup item and ticks each one off as it's done. The AD's Today screen shows the next four.

School Setup › Import, or Settings › School › Import program from CSV, takes levels, sports, coaches, teams, rosters (name and grade only), events and budget lines. Download the sample CSV files from that screen; they're designed to import together, in that order.

The iPhone app

Install it and sign in with the same Google account. It shows Today, Mail, Calendar, Equipment and More › People, plus home-screen widgets for the brief, tasks, calendar and notes.

Using AD Focus

How the app is organised, and how it behaves when you work with your team.

Press ⌘K in the app to jump anywhere, or ⇧⌘M to see every screen as a tile.

Today and the morning brief

Today shows one line for each area that needs action: weather, officials, transport, clearances, certifications, facilities, budget review, urgent mail and follow-ups. Each line opens the screen where it's fixed.

The brief is prepared overnight and arrives as a 7:15 notification; change this in Settings › Workday › Notifications.

Working with your team

When your school is connected, changes appear on teammates' screens within about a second, and their initials show on records they have open.

If two people change the same field, the later save wins and both see a short notice. A field you're typing in is never overwritten: an Updated by … badge offers Use theirs or Keep mine.

Locks and sign-off

In Trip Risk, one person edits a section at a time, and others see who is editing it. The lock is released about 45 seconds after that person leaves, quits or is idle for two minutes.

A signed-off assessment is read-only until someone clicks Reopen… and gives a reason; it then needs approval again.

Nothing goes out on its own

Notices, emails and calendar changes are drafts or previews that you confirm. AI only runs when you click, and student names are hidden from it by default.

The one exception is a scheduled survey with automatic sending switched on, both in Settings and on the survey.

Customising the app

Settings › Appearance sets school colours, font, app icon and the sidebar arrangement; Use Recommended Layout puts the sidebar back to the six areas.

The AD chooses which modules the school uses in Settings › School › Your department.

Data, sync and backups

Every save is logged, so almost any mistake can be undone, and a move file carries everything to a new Mac.

Undo and restore

Edit › Undo (⌘Z) steps back through your own saves, skipping any field a teammate has changed since.

Settings › History lists every save with before and after, and can restore deleted records. History is kept for at least 30 days.

Syncing

A connected Mac sends your changes moments after you stop typing and receives teammates' changes live. Settings › Team › Sync Now forces a check.

Offline, the app keeps working and shows Reconnecting… then Catching up… when the connection returns.

Moving to a new Mac

  1. On the old Mac, choose Settings › Data › Create Move File… and pick a passphrase of at least 12 characters. It can't be recovered if forgotten.
  2. Send the file with Send with AirDrop…. It lands in Downloads on the new Mac.
  3. On the new Mac, choose Settings › Data › Set Up from a Move File…, type the passphrase, then click Restore and Restart. The app walks you through the Mac permissions again, because macOS doesn't let them move between Macs.
  4. Back on the old Mac, click Finish Moving… so only the new Mac sends surveys and waiting changes.

Any data that was already on the new Mac is kept as a backup for 30 days after the move.

Student records and retention

The AD can erase a student's records (typing the name to confirm), place a legal hold that blocks erasure, and switch on automatic deletion by category.

Deletion runs nightly and skips students under a hold. Every export, hold and erasure is written to the school's privacy log.

Removing data

  • Settings › Data › Erase all data… clears this Mac's AD Focus data. You can bring it back from History. Calendars and Things are not touched.
  • Settings › Privacy › Remove All Data from This Mac… (type REMOVE to confirm) signs out of Google and your school, then on restart deletes all local data, backups, caches, saved keys and settings. Use it before handing a Mac on.
  • Delete School… (AD only, typing the school name to confirm) deletes the school's shared data for everyone. It cannot be undone.

What AD Focus never stores

Rosters hold name and grade (plus school ID, school email and equipment sizes for equipment records). Health information is limited to clearance yes/no flags with expiry dates and minimal incident facts.

AD Focus never stores diagnoses, medications, medical notes, bank or tax numbers, or uploaded forms.

Troubleshooting

Most problems come down to a different Google account, a module that's switched off or not in your role, or the school's licence. Check those first.

macOS says AD Focus can't be opened

Why macOS checks a newly downloaded app before its first launch.

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, then open AD Focus again.

A screen is missing from the sidebar

Why Your school switched that module off, or your role doesn't include it.

Look in All Modules (⇧⌘M). If it isn't there, ask your AD to check Settings › School › Your department and Settings › Team › Feature Access…. The Incident Log is switched on separately, in Settings › Policies › Modules.

A screen or record is read-only

Why Your role is view-only there, someone else is editing that Trip Risk section, the item is signed off, or the licence has lapsed.

Read the banner. Wait for the other person to finish (about 45 seconds after they leave), use Reopen…, or ask your AD.

"Sign in with Google first", or no invitation appears

Why You signed in with a different Google account from the one invited.

In Settings › Team, sign out and sign in with the invited address, or ask your AD to invite the email you use.

Joining a school only offers "Replace This Mac's Data"

Why This Mac already holds another school's data, and one Mac never mixes schools.

If you need a copy, make a move file first (Settings › Data), then replace.

Teammates' changes don't appear

Why You're offline, the Mac was asleep, or your sign-in expired.

Check Wi-Fi, then Settings › Team › Sync Now. If asked, click Sign In Again. Unsent work is kept and sent when you reconnect.

A notice says you replaced someone's change

Why Two people edited the same field at nearly the same time.

This is expected: the later save wins. Check the value and change it back if needed.

macOS keeps asking for your keychain password

Why AD Focus keeps your sign-ins in the Mac keychain.

Enter your Mac password and click Always Allow.

Calendar, Reminders or Contacts information doesn't show

Why The permission was declined.

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, choose the matching item, and switch on AD Focus.

Events don't appear in Google Calendar

Why AD Focus only writes to linked calendars with publishing switched on, and only after you confirm the preview.

In Settings › Calendars, link the calendar and switch publishing on.

"Couldn't open Mail" when making a draft

Why No default mail app is set up on this Mac.

Use Copy for Gmail / Outlook, or Save PDF and attach it yourself.

Every window says "AD Focus is locked"

Why App lock turned on after idle time or display sleep.

Click Unlock and use Touch ID or your Mac password. Change the timing in Settings › Privacy › App lock.

Lost the phone with your two-step sign-in codes

Why Your only authenticator app was on that phone.

Use your second authenticator if you set one up; otherwise email support to reset it.

"Couldn't get exchange rates"

Why The currency service couldn't be reached.

Enter the exchange rate by hand.

"Close other AD Focus windows and try again"

Why Another window has the same record open.

Close the other AD Focus windows and try again.

Can't invite anyone: "All seats in use"

Why Every seat on the school's licence is taken.

Remove someone who has left (Mark as Left), or contact us to add seats.

You can view but not change anything, with a licence warning

Why The school's licence has ended and its grace period is over.

Renew the licence. Nothing is lost, and you can still view and export everything meanwhile.

"Already used your free trial" when creating a school

Why Each Google account gets one free trial.

Contact us about a licence.

Questions and getting help

Quick answers, and how to reach us.

Does AD Focus send emails or post anything by itself?

No. Every notice, email and calendar change is a draft or preview you confirm. The only exception is a scheduled survey with automatic sending switched on.

Does AI see student names?

Not by default. AI runs only when you click, shows what it will send, and hides student names. Staff notes and coach evaluations are never sent. Choose your provider in Settings › AI, or use the free on-device model.

Can coaches see staff notes or evaluations?

No. Staff Notes are for the AD only by default, and the AD shares an evaluation with a coach one checkpoint at a time.

Can I use AD Focus without connecting to a school?

Yes. It works on one Mac on its own; connecting to a school adds sharing and live updates.

Can I belong to two schools?

Not on the same Mac. Joining a second school replaces this Mac's data.

Is my Gmail shared with my team?

No. Mail stays on your own Mac. Recent and starred mail is kept on the Mac, encrypted, so you can read it offline.

What happens at the end of the school year?

Choose File › School Setup… › Move to a New Year. For blackouts, teams, coaches, rosters (grades move up and graduates leave), budget, practices, events and goals, choose Keep, Change or Leave behind. Expenses, clearances, trips, incidents, tournaments and equipment never carry over.

Where is the setting for…?

Settings has its own search field. Type a word such as "time zone" or "backup".

How do I report a problem?

Email myworlddiscovered@gmail.com with:

  1. Your name, school and role.
  2. What you did, what you expected, and what happened, including the exact wording of any message.
  3. A screenshot (⇧⌘4), with any student details covered.
  4. Your Mac model and macOS version (Apple menu › About This Mac).

Good to know

  • Never send passwords, two-step codes, move files or their passphrases.
